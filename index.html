About Us

Neotech Enterprise is a professionally managed diagnostic and healthcare products distribution firm based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. We specialize in supplying biomedical and pathology instruments, reagents, and diagnostic kits to pathology laboratories, hospitals, and diagnostic centers.

With more than a decade of combined experience in the diagnostics and pharmaceutical industry, we focus on delivering quality products, timely service support, and long-term partnerships with our clients and principals.

We work as authorized channel partners for leading diagnostic companies and provide complete solutions covering Clinical Chemistry, Immunology, Hematology, and Coagulation.

Vision & Mission

Vision:
To associate with diagnostic and healthcare companies as long-term channel partners and grow mutually through trust, performance, and professional ethics.

Mission:
To provide quality diagnostic products, timely delivery, and reliable service support by utilizing our rich experience in the diagnostics and healthcare industry.
